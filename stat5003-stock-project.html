What this dataset is

It is a daily market panel for current S&P 500 constituents, paired with a market benchmark and company reference table — not a single-stock price file.

Use for modelling

Open, High, Low, Close, Adj Close, Volume, S&P500, Sector, and Industry. These create returns, volatility, range, drawdown, volume, and market-regime features.

Keep as context or exclude

Date and Symbol define the panel. Names, exchange, and location help describe coverage. Current-price and current-fundamental fields must not be backfilled into historical predictions.

Data quality is part of the research

The stock table is a complete Date × Symbol grid. Many cells represent periods where reliable prices are unavailable.

67.3% of grid rows lack OHLCV

Each of the six market-value columns has 1,273,705 missing cells. About 617,831 stock-day rows contain complete market values.

Missing · 67.3%Complete · 32.7%

Do not hide this

Missing does not automatically mean random.

IPO dates, ticker histories, and source availability can create structured missingness. Filtering rules must be reported, not treated as an invisible cleanup step.

Limits to carry into every result

A good report names what the data cannot establish as clearly as what it can.

  • Survivorship bias: the universe reflects current constituents, not the historical membership of the S&P 500.
  • Snapshot leakage: current company values cannot be used as if they were known at every historical prediction date.
  • Stale endpoint: despite the title, Version 1023 stops in December 2024.
  • Scope: findings describe this selected constituent universe, not all US equities or a profitable trading strategy.

这个数据集到底是什么

它是当前S&P 500成分股的日度市场面板，配合大盘基准和公司参考表；并不是单只股票的价格文件。

可用于建模的特征来源

不能隐藏的问题

缺失值不一定是随机缺失。

IPO日期、股票代码历史和数据源可用性都可能形成结构性缺失。筛选规则必须在报告中呈现，不能作为不可见的清洗步骤。

所有结论都要保留的限制

好的报告不仅说明数据能证明什么，也清楚说明它不能证明什么。

  • 幸存者偏差：股票范围反映当前成分股，而不是每个历史时点的真实S&P 500成员。
  • 快照信息泄漏：不能把当前公司数值当作每个历史预测日期都已知的信息。
  • 更新终点：虽然标题写着每日更新，Version 1023实际截止于2024年12月。
  • 适用范围：结果只描述所选成分股范围，不代表所有美股或可盈利交易策略。

02 · Research proposal

Can we flag next month's volatility?

A forward-looking classification study designed around the part of market behaviour that is more persistent than return direction: volatility.

Current design state

Structured, not final

The prediction contract is ready for a reproducible audit. The threshold and binary-versus-three-class decision remain open.

Working research question
Can historical price, return, volume, market, and sector information classify whether an S&P 500 stock will experience high volatility over the next 20 trading days?

This is a risk-identification question, not a claim that the model can predict stock direction or generate trading profit.

The prediction contract

Every row needs an explicit prediction date, an information boundary, and a future outcome window.

T · MONTH END

Observation

One stock on the final common trading day of a calendar month.

→
KNOWN BY T

Predictors

Only lagged price, return, volatility, volume, market, and safe company-context information.

→
T+1 … T+20

Outcome

Realised volatility calculated from the next 20 trading-day returns.

RV20 = sd(rt+1, …, rt+20) × √252

Provisional binary label

High risk if annualised RV20 > 30%; otherwise Not high risk. The 30% line is a working threshold, not a final course decision.

Why 30% remains provisional

A useful threshold must create meaningful risk classes without becoming either trivial or too rare to model.

01Count the classes

Calculate exact High / Not high counts for the full eligible panel and for each chronological split.

02Compare 25 / 30 / 35%

Inspect class balance, year stability, and sector composition under each fixed threshold.

03Check training quantiles

Compare domain thresholds with cut-offs estimated from training data only — never from the test set.

04Stress-test time regimes

Verify that a threshold remains meaningful in calm and turbulent market periods.

Candidate feature families

Features are engineered within each stock and use only information available at or before the prediction date.

Return & trend

Lagged 5/20/60-day returns, moving-average position, momentum, rolling highs.

Volatility & range

5/20/60-day realised volatility, intraday range, drawdown, Bollinger width.

Volume & liquidity

Relative volume, volume growth, rolling volume variability, price-volume correlation.

Market regime

S&P 500 return, volatility, drawdown, and moving-average state at time t.

Company context

Sector and Industry for group comparison or encoding, with a sensitivity check for classification drift.

Explicit exclusions

Future returns, future ranges, current snapshot fundamentals used backward in time, and any full-data preprocessing.

Modelling protocol

Financial prediction needs a stricter validation design than an ordinary randomly split tabular dataset.

Chronological split

Train on earlier years, tune on a later validation period, and evaluate once on the latest test period.

20-day embargo

Remove observations whose future label window crosses a train, validation, or test boundary.

Training-only preprocessing

Fit imputation, scaling, encoding, feature selection, and target cut-offs on training data only.

Evaluation hierarchy

Prioritise Macro-F1, Balanced Accuracy, high-risk Recall, PR-AUC, and the confusion matrix; keep Accuracy secondary.

02 · 研究方案

我们能提前识别
下个月的高波动吗？

这是一个前瞻性分类研究，选择比收益方向更具有持续性的市场行为：波动率。

当前设计状态

结构已确定，定义未最终确定

预测问题已经可以进入可复现审计，但阈值和二分类/三分类选择仍然开放。

暂定研究问题
历史价格、收益率、成交量、大盘环境和行业信息，能否判断一只S&P 500股票在未来20个交易日是否会出现高波动？

这是风险识别问题，不是宣称模型能够准确预测涨跌或产生交易利润。

预测问题的明确约定
